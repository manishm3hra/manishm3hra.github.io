Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Manish Mehra ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information in connection with our mobile applications, including PDF321: Compress, Arrange & Merge, WPM Touch Typing, RooTree, and other productivity tools.

1. Information Collection and Use

To provide a better experience, while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that we request will be retained on your device and is not collected by us in any way unless explicitly stated (e.g., cloud sync).

2. Third-Party Services

Our apps use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. We use these partners for advertising, payments, and analytics. Please refer to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob & AdSense
  • Firebase (Auth, Analytics, Crashlytics)
  • Meta (Facebook) Audience Network
  • Unity Ads
  • AppLovin
  • InMobi

3. Account & Payments

Firebase Authentication: We use Google Sign-In to allow you to create an account and protect your progress. Your email and unique User ID are stored securely.

Google Play Billing: All financial transactions are handled securely through the Google Play Store. We do not store or have access to your credit card or banking information.

In-App Credit Packs & Device Retention: Consumable credit packs (e.g., PDF321 credit packs) are hardware-encrypted and bound to your active device installation. Credits never expire on your active device and are preserved during app reinstalls on the same device via encrypted auto-backup. However, consumable credit balances do not transfer across different physical devices.

4. Advertising and Analytics

We use AdMob and its mediation partners (Meta, Unity, AppLovin, InMobi) to display advertisements. These partners may use device identifiers to personalize ads. We also use Google Analytics to monitor app performance and improve user experience.

5. Data Deletion Rights

In compliance with Google Play's policies, you have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated data. You can perform this action in-app via the Settings menu or by visiting our Data Deletion Page and following the instructions.

6. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
