Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This privacy policy applies to all mobile applications (the "Apps") developed and published by amjack on the Google Play Store.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not directly collect, store, or share any personal identity information from our users. However, our Apps utilize third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) that may collect information used to identify your device, analyze app performance, or serve personalized advertisements.

2. Third-Party Services We Use

Below are the third-party service providers used in our Apps, along with links to their respective privacy policies, which govern how they handle your data:

  • Google Play Services: Used to run system features inside our Android apps.
    Google Play Services Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers (like the Google Advertising ID) to show relevant ads.
    AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics: Used to collect anonymous usage data (e.g., which screens you visit, how long you use the app) to help us improve the user experience.
    Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Remote Config: Used to change the behavior and appearance of our apps dynamically without requiring users to download an app update.
    Firebase Privacy Policy

3. Cookies and Device Identifiers

Device identifiers are unique identifiers allocated to your device. The third-party tools listed above automatically access these identifiers to measure app installations, track user behavior, and serve targeted ads. You can opt-out of ad personalization or reset your advertising ID within your Android device's system settings.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will delete it immediately.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any changes made are effective immediately upon being posted to this URL.
