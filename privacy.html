3. Cookies and Device Identifiers

Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) are unique identifiers allocated to your device. The third-party tools listed above automatically access these identifiers to measure app installations, track user behavior for diagnostics, and serve targeted ads. You can opt-out of ad personalization or reset your advertising ID within your Android device's system settings.

Our Apps are not designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will delete it immediately.
