Account & Data Deletion

At Amjack Apps, we value your privacy. In compliance with Google Play's Data Safety policies, we provide a simple way for users to delete their accounts and all associated cloud-stored data.

What data will be deleted?
Requesting deletion will permanently erase your unique User ID, email address, cloud-synced test credits, and any saved subscription status from our servers.

How to request deletion:

  1. In-App: Open the app, go to Settings, and click "Delete My Account & Cloud Data" at the bottom.
  2. Via Web: If you have already uninstalled the app, click the button below to send a deletion request via email. Please use the email address associated with your Google account.
